# Business Insights and Recommendations

Scope: delivered orders, Sep 2016 to Aug 2018. Revenue = sum of customer payments.

| # | Observation | Interpretation | Business impact / Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 96,477 delivered orders, R$ 15.42M revenue, average order value R$ 159.86. Orders peak at about 7,300 in Nov 2017, then stay at about 6,000 to 7,000 a month. | Strong growth, a holiday spike, then a plateau. | Plan stock and logistics before November. Find new customers or categories to grow past the plateau. |
| 2 | SP brings 37% of revenue; SP, RJ and MG together bring 63%. | Demand is concentrated in the south-east. | Test marketing and local sellers in RS, PR and SC. |
| 3 | health_beauty (R$ 1.23M), watches_gifts (R$ 1.17M) and bed_bath_table (R$ 1.02M) lead on revenue. bed_bath_table has the most orders but a lower review (3.92). | Popular does not always mean well rated. | Promote health_beauty and sports_leisure. Investigate quality and delivery in bed_bath_table. |
| 4 | Only 2,801 of 93,357 customers (3.0%) ordered more than once, and they bring 5.6% of revenue. | Almost everyone buys once. | Use follow-up emails, coupons and loyalty points to bring customers back. |
| 5 | Most customers spend R$ 50 to 200 in total; only 4,265 spend R$ 500 or more. | The customer base is mid-value. | Offer bundles and free-shipping thresholds. Treat top spenders as VIPs. |
| 6 | Average delivery is 12.56 days, and 8.1% of orders are late. RR, AP and AM take 26 to 29 days, against 8.8 in SP. AL has the highest late share (about 24%). | Speed depends on distance, and some states miss their promised date far more often than others. | Add regional carriers for the north. Set realistic estimates, and review AL's carrier performance. |
| 7 | Review score by delay: on time 4.29, 1 to 3 days late 3.77, 4 to 7 days late 2.32, 8+ days late 1.73. | Late delivery is the biggest driver of bad reviews. | Fix the 8+ day delays first (3,252 orders). That is the fastest way to raise the 4.16 average. |
| 8 | Top sellers earn R$ 187K to 227K each, mostly in SP. Some sellers with 30+ orders have reviews below 3.0. | Seller quality varies widely. | Set a minimum rating, coach low-rated sellers and reward top performers. |

## Overall recommendation
1. Fix late deliveries first, because they hurt reviews most.
2. Bring customers back for a second order.
3. Reduce dependence on SP by growing the next strongest states.
4. Plan every year for the November peak.
